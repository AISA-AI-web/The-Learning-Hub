      Staff explorer

      Everyone, one row each

      Everyone in the selection: the roster plus anyone who has used the Hub. Click a column to sort, a name to see that person on their own. Days, page views and issues count the chosen dates; modules count everything finished by the end of them.

      How this is counted. A “Hub user” is a signed-in staff account with at least one page view, sign-in or module event. “Active” means at least one page view or module event in the chosen dates, and a “day active” is an Abu Dhabi calendar day with a page view. A completion is the first time a person finishes a module; “opened” is a view of the module’s page (or a module event, for the one module without a page of its own). Newsletter traffic sources come from the browser’s referrer: Gmail on the web reports itself as google.com, and most email apps send none. Devices come from the browser’s user agent, so an iPad asking for the desktop site counts as a laptop. Readiness themes are keyword matches on teachers’ own words, so treat them as a guide.
