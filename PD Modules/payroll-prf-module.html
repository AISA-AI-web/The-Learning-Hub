Section 2

Payroll Basics — Definition & the Three Activities

📖 What "payroll" means

Payroll is the net amount of money paid on a monthly basis to an employee for their earned salaries, allowances, stipends, reimbursements and deductions. As a function, it is the process by which employees receive their salary.

For you, payroll breaks down into three operating activities:

🏦
One-time

1. Submit bank account details

Done once, as soon as possible after you join.

⚙️
Monthly

2. Processing & payment

Your salary is calculated and transferred each month.

📨
Monthly

3. Pay slip distribution

Your pay slip is emailed to you after each pay month.

We'll walk through each of these in turn. The only one that needs action from you up front is the first.

Section 3 · Your First Action

Step 1: Submit Your Bank Account Details

A one-time task — do this as soon as possible so your salary can be paid.

Who

Completed by you, the employee.

When

As soon as possible after joining.

Where

To the HR / Accounts Office, using the AISA Bank Account Form (Annex A).

How

Submit the completed Bank Account Form to the HR Office.

⚖️ Why it's required

UAE law requires it. Under the Ministry of Human Resources & Emiratisation (MoHRE) Wages Protection System (WPS), salaries must be paid in dirhams and transferred to your UAE bank account. No valid local account on file means your salary can't be processed.

🏦 What the bank needs to open your account

1. Bank form(s) for opening the account
2. Emirates ID
3. Mobile phone number

Section 4

How & When Payroll Is Processed and Paid

Payroll is managed by the Accounts Office. The actual transfer of money to your bank account is made through the WPS by the Central Bank, based on approved documents and in line with your contract and statutory requirements.

📅 Monthly payment timing

  • 1st year: August to May — paid at the end of each month.
  • 2nd year onward: August to July — paid at the end of each month.
  • June: 50% paid; the rest of July's balance follows.
  • July: paid on the last working day of the year; the July balance is settled by the end of August.

🗂️ Document processing windows

  • Aug–Nov: 15th to 22nd of the month.
  • December: 5th to 7th of the month.
  • Jan–May: 15th to 22nd of the month.
  • June: 10th to 12th of the month.

Any change to your pay (stipend, leave, reimbursement) needs its document in before that month's window to land in the same pay run.

Section 6

The Purchase Requisition Form (PRF)

A Purchase Requisition Form is an internal control document showing that a particular purchase of goods and/or services is duly authorized. To be valid it must be signed by the Principal, the School Director, and the Business Manager (Annex B).

🏪 1. School pays the supplier directly

Attach to the PRF:

  • • The invoice
  • • The supplier's bank account details

💳 2. Reimbursement through your payroll

Attach to the PRF:

  • • The invoice / proof of the charge
  • • Proof of payment you made
  • • The AISA PD application form, if using Professional Development money

💡 Tip

Reimbursements feed into payroll, so a PRF submitted after the month's document-processing window will be paid in the next pay run. Submit early.

Section 7

Quick Reference & Who to Contact

✅ Do

  • • Submit your Bank Account Form (Annex A) to HR straight away.
  • • Use a UAE dirham bank account for WPS.
  • • Check your pay slip each month against what you expect.
  • • Get PRF signatures and attachments in before the monthly window.

🚫 Don't

  • • Expect payment before your bank details are on file.
  • • Submit a PRF without all three approval signatures.
  • • Forget the invoice and proof of payment on a reimbursement.
  • • Make verbal-only requests for anything that needs a paper trail.
