Section 1

From Job Offer to Residence Visa — the Whole Journey

Getting your UAE work authorization is a sequence of seven steps, handled mostly by the AISA Government Relations (PRO) team. This module walks you through each one so you know what's happening, what's needed from you, and roughly how long each step takes.

  3. 3Ministry of Interior — Immigration (Entry) Visa2 days
  4. 4Unemployment Insurance (ILOE)same day
  5. 5Blood Test (clinic truck)2 days

A note on timings

The day-counts shown are typical processing times for each step under normal conditions — not a promise. Government systems and queues vary. Your PRO will keep you updated as each step clears.

Section 2 · Steps 1–3

Ministry of Labour & Immigration Approvals

1

Ministry of Labour — Job Offer

2 working days

You'll review and sign the official MOL job offer. Read it carefully — it sets out:

Personal information Salary & benefits Housing Summer leave Labour articles Your signature
2

Ministry of Labour — Approval

within 4 working days

The Ministry reviews and approves the signed offer. No action needed from you — your PRO submits and tracks this.

3

Ministry of Interior — Immigration (Entry) Visa

2 working days

The Ministry of Interior issues your immigration / entry visa approval — the permit that lets your residency process continue inside the UAE.

Section 3 · Step 4 · Action needed

Unemployment Insurance (ILOE)

A mandatory subscription you must complete before the next step.

Unemployment Insurance is a UAE government scheme that provides temporary financial support to employees who lose their jobs for reasons beyond their control. It pays 60% of your basic salary for up to three months.

💰

Policy fee

AED 126

covered by the employee

📋

Status

Mandatory

required to proceed

⏱️

Timing

Same day

quick to complete

⚠️ Why it matters

This is a mandatory subscription — your visa cannot move to the next stage until it's done. The AED 126 fee is paid by you, the employee.

Section 4 · Steps 5–6

Medical Test & Emirates ID

5

Blood Test by Clinic Truck

2 working days

A mobile clinic visits for the standard UAE residency medical screening, which tests for:

HIV Tuberculosis Hepatitis C
6

Emirates ID

5 days after fingerprints

Your Emirates ID is the single most important document in the UAE. You'll give fingerprints, and the card is issued about five working days later. You'll use it for almost everything:

🏦 Banking 🩺 Medical insurance 🚗 UAE driving licence 📱 Cell phone & WiFi 🏛️ Government offices 🏨 Clubs & hotels
Section 5 · Step 7 · Final step

Employment Visa Sticker

The last step — your residency is now complete.

The visa sticker is the final step of the process. A few important things to know about it:

📅

Your visa and Emirates ID are valid for 2 years and expire on the same day.

📕

The visa is not placed in your passport — it's digital.

📲

Install the ICP UAE app on your smartphone to access and download your visa sticker copy.

⏱️

This step typically takes about 1 working day.

✅ You're all set

Once the sticker is issued, you're a fully documented UAE resident. Keep your Emirates ID and the ICP app handy — and note both renewal dates two years out.

Section 6

Your PRO — Duties & How They Can Help

The Government Relations Officer (PRO) handles AISA's dealings with government entities — and is your go-to person for visa and residency matters. Beyond your own visa, the PRO can help with:

🏛️Processing all school-related transactions with government entities
📜Renewing school licences with the municipalities
🪪Issuing, renewing and cancelling visas
👨‍👩‍👧Helping issue family visas (children and nannies)
⚖️Handling procedures at police stations and courts — police cases, marriage procedures, accident reports, car-repair approvals and clearing fines

🤝 Reach out any time

You may contact the PRO for assistance with any government-related transaction. If you're unsure who your PRO is or how to reach them, ask the HR Office — they'll point you to the right person.
