Section 2 · Important

AISA Is a High-Risk Entity — Safety Is Shared

Abu Dhabi classifies schools as either High-Risk or Medium-Risk entities. The classification is based on:

Nature & complexity of operations (tasks/activities)
Number of employees (excluding contractors)
Maximum number of other persons (students & contractors)
Use / handling of hazardous materials & wastes
Complexity of buildings & associated structures
Entity land area & number of locations

🛡️ What this means for you

AISA is classified as High-Risk. Being a High-Risk Entity means health and safety is a shared responsibility between all staff, students, contractors, and even visitors.

During audits, the audit team may approach you to check that you're aware of your school's High-Risk status. Now you are.

📣 How OSH responsibilities reach you

Every employee is responsible for working safely, following procedures, and reporting hazards. HR communicates Occupational Safety & Health (OSH) roles, responsibilities and accountabilities through three channels:

Section 5

Traffic Management, Parking Zones & Gate Timings

Arrival and dismissal are the busiest, highest-risk moments of the day. Parking is colour-coded into four zones, and each gate has a defined use, time window and speed limit.

🅿️ The four parking zones

Green — Parents
Red — Staff
Yellow — Buses
Purple — Overflow

🌅 Morning drop-off · 7:25–8:00 AM

  • Buses use the Bus Loop / Yellow Zone, drop at the curb and exit immediately. No parents in the Bus Loop.
  • Parents use the Parent Lane / Green Zone as a one-way Kiss-and-Go — drop curb-side, don't park or exit the car, then move on.
  • Staff/visitors use Staff Parking / Red Zone; visitors check in at the main gate first.

🌆 Afternoon pick-up · 2:50–3:10 PM

  • Buses load at the Yellow Zone and depart strictly on schedule; missed-bus students go to a safe waiting area.
  • Parents queue in the Green Zone; staff escort students to the car. No student crosses the Parent Lane unattended — slow loads go to Overflow / Purple parking.
  • Pedestrians exit only through supervised pedestrian gates and walkways.

Section 6

General Safety in Your Classroom & Corridors

🚪 Housekeeping

Corridors, stairwells and fire-exit doors must remain completely unobstructed at all times.

⚠️ Trip & fall prevention

Report trailing cables, loose mats or damaged flooring immediately. Wet areas during cleaning must be marked with yellow warning signs.

🪜 Working at height

Never stand on student desks, chairs or spinning office chairs to hang decorations. Use an approved step ladder or log a request with maintenance.

🔌 Electrical & chemical

Use electrical equipment safely and report damaged items. Where applicable, handle, store and dispose of hazardous substances safely.

🛠️ Work order requests

Submit all maintenance, repair, furniture setup, moving and facilities requests through the Workflow Work Order Request Form so they're tracked, assigned and completed on time. Avoid verbal-only requests unless it's an emergency.

Section 7 · Critical

Emergency Procedures — Fire, Evacuation & Lockdown

🔥 Fire detection & extinguishers

Campus facilities are monitored by automated fire alarms connected directly to Abu Dhabi Civil Defence via the Hassantuk framework. Only use a fire extinguisher if all four are true:

✔ You're trained to do so ✔ You know the classification ✔ It is not spreading rapidly ✔ You have a clear escape path

🚨 Evacuation (continuous siren)

  • 1. Cease instruction immediately; students leave bags behind.
  • 2. Guide the class calmly along the route on the evacuation map.
  • 3. Go to your assigned assembly zone — e.g. the Football Pitch.
  • 4. Take a headcount against your roster; signal your sector marshal with status cards — Green (all present) or Red (missing or extra students).

🔒 Lockdown (PA announcement)

For an external campus threat, a lockdown directive is broadcast over the PA. Immediately:

  • • Pull students in from corridors
  • • Lock doors, close blinds, turn off lights
  • • Have students sit quietly out of sight-lines
  • • Wait for the official "All Clear" announcement
