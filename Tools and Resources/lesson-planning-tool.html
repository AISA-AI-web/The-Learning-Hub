How to use this tool effectively for Lesson Planning

  • Focus on Depth, Not Breadth: Effective planning focuses on depth. Avoid ticking every box; instead, select only the 3-5 strategies that form the absolute core focus of this specific lesson.
  • Align with Best Practices: The reference codes (e.g., 1.3.4) map to national educational standards, ensuring our daily classroom practices align seamlessly with top-tier teaching expectations.
  • Prioritize the "Big Two": Differentiation (3.1.4) and Critical Thinking (3.1.5) are universally recognized as the strongest drivers of student achievement. Ensure these are planned for and evidenced frequently.
  • Document the Invisible Work: Use the grey "Broader Context" box to record the behind-the-scenes data analysis and marking that makes your lesson successful, providing a complete picture of your pedagogical planning.
