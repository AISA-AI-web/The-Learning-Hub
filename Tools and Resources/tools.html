About this Module

An essential guide for teachers and parents to understand how MAP Growth works, what RIT scores mean, and how to use the data to support student learning.

🧩 What is MAP Growth?

MAP Growth is not a traditional standardized test. It is an adaptive assessment designed to measure a student's academic growth over time, regardless of their grade level.

  • ✓ If a student answers correctly, the next question gets harder.
  • ✓ If they answer incorrectly, the next question gets easier.

📊 Understanding RIT Scores

The result of the test is a RIT Score (Rasch Unit). Think of this like a ruler for academic growth.

  • ➜ It is an equal-interval scale (like inches on a ruler).
  • ➜ It tracks progress from year to year, not just within a single grade.
  • ➜ It connects directly to instructional areas students are ready to learn.

Setting Up Testing Sessions

ℹ️
Admin needs to first make sure the IT team has rosters ready and teachers are able to set up testing sessions.
  1. Log in to NWEA Online.
  2. Click "MAP Growth".
  3. Click "Manage Testing Sessions".
  4. Click "Find Students to Test" — fill in all dropdowns (do not fill in text boxes), then click "Search".
  5. Uncheck students who will not be testing with you. Refer to the SSP student list to know who needs accommodations and who will be pulled out. Talk to Student Services if anything needs to change.
  6. Click "Add Students" — select all students who will be testing WITH YOU.
  7. Click "Assign Test" — select tests to assign (see table below).

Goal Setting

Setting goals with students before benchmark testing helps them take ownership of their learning, focus on specific areas of growth, and approach the assessment with a clear purpose. It builds motivation, reduces anxiety, and gives students a sense of direction — transforming the test from a measure of performance into an opportunity for progress and reflection.

The Day of the Test

Setup, proctoring protocol, recovery from issues, and make-up workflow.

🪑

Testing Environment

Ensure the testing environment is appropriate.

  • ✓Spread out desks.
  • ✓Remove or cover materials that might aid students during the test.
!

MAP Protocol for Missing Supplies

Headphones, updated iPad — students may NOT take the MAP test without proper materials. They should have separate work to complete independently and without distracting others.

Teachers should message individual families reminding them of the expectations.

🚦

Before Testing Begins

🤫

"Shh We are Testing" sign

Post on your door before students arrive.

📓

Non-tech tasks ready

For students who finish early — quiet, no iPads.

  1. Log in to NWEA Online.
  2. Click "MAP Growth".
  3. Click "Manage Testing Sessions".
  4. Under "Saved Testing Sessions" find your session. Click "Test Now".
  5. Write your session name and password on the board for students — then students wait.
  6. Read the script from page 10 of the Proctor Manual.
  7. Once all students are logged in and ready, click "Confirm" to start testing.
👀

During Testing

  • ✓Actively proctor the entire session — teachers should not be sitting behind their desks while students are testing.
  • ✓Ensure and facilitate a quiet, focused, encouraging environment.
📚

After Testing

  • ✓Students work quietly on non-tech tasks at their desks — no iPads can be used, outside of taking the MAP test.
💡

Important to Know

📍
If students are guessing or answering rapidly, the system will alert you and you'll need to type the proctor PIN on the student's iPad.

Never use

Do not ever use these options.

🛠️

If the iPad app freezes

Exit the app and restart. If pressing the Home button fails to exit, press Home + Power at the same time until the iPad restarts. On your proctor computer, let the student back into the test. Restart the app and ask the student to sign in again.

Retaking MAP Testing

The Leadership Team will pull a report after the first week of testing is complete, and share this with your entire team.

📉

Dropped 10+ RIT points

Have those students retake the assessment.

⚡

Rapid guessing ≥ 10%

If it significantly impacted the score, have them retake.

🛠️ Admin to-do

After students have done a retake, both scores will be registered in MAP. We need to hide the lower test score from showing up on reports:

  1. Open Rostering.
  2. Click Manage Students.
  3. Search the student name.
  4. Click View/Update Test Events.
  5. Sort by "Start date".
  6. Select the lower test score on the duplicate test and click "Exclude from Reports".
