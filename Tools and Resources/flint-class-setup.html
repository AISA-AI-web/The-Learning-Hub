If something goes wrong

I can't see + Add a class.Check you're signed in with your AISA account. If it's still missing, contact the AI & Innovation team.
A student says the link doesn't work.Make sure they're signed in to Flint with their AISA school account. If it still fails, open your class Settings and check access isn't set to Private.
A student joined the wrong class.Contact the AI & Innovation team for help removing them.
My students need help getting started.Share Flint's Get started as a student article.
