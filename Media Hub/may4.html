What's New on the Learning Hub

A round-up of the digital tools, AI Gems, and Learning Hub upgrades the T&L team has shipped for the AISA teaching staff.

A note from T&L

Welcome to the very first Digital Tools newsletter. From this issue onward, we'll use this space to keep teachers in the loop on the AI tools, Gemini Gems, and Learning Hub upgrades we've shipped — what's new, what it does, and how to use it in your classroom tomorrow morning. Here's what landed this round.

New Tool · Trial

Chalkie.ai trial — yours through the end of the year

We've secured an AISA-wide Chalkie.ai trial that runs until the end of the school year. Chalkie is an AI-powered teaching assistant for planning, content creation, and personalising learning. Think Magic School-style assistant with a focus on lesson design and student-friendly outputs.

  • ✓ Card now live on the Learning Hub under Tools & Resources → AI Teaching Assistants.
  • ✓ Sign in with your AISA account at chalkie.ai to access the trial.
  • ✓ Try it for one lesson this week and tell us how it goes — your feedback shapes whether we keep it past June.

Built In-House

IB Exam Format Generator (Gemini Gem)

We designed a custom Gemini Gem that takes any content you share — a passage, a topic, a unit of work — and returns a downloadable PDF formatted to look exactly like an authentic IB exam paper. It's a fast way to give students realistic exposure to IB layout, instruction language, and question framing without rebuilding a paper from scratch.

  • ✓ Card now live on the Learning Hub under Tools & Resources → Assessment & Test Prep.
  • ✓ Drop in any source material — paragraphs, source documents, or learning outcomes.
  • ✓ Output is a print-ready PDF that mirrors IB paper layout for student practice.

👏 Shoutouts

  • ★ Will Lester — for requesting an IB-format generator in the first place. This one started with your ask.
  • ★ Shawn Carder — for the spark of using LaTeX under the hood to render the downloadable PDFs. That's what gives the output its authentic IB paper look.

Hub Upgrade

A search bar across the entire Learning Hub

The Learning Hub landing page now has a hub-wide search directory. One search box. Find any module, AI tool, library resource, committee, or orientation page in one keystroke — no more hunting through tabs.

  • ✓ Type a keyword (e.g. "MAP", "ethics", "twinkl", "viva") and the directory surfaces matching pages and tools.
  • ✓ Keyboard shortcut: ⌘ K / Ctrl K to jump straight to it.
  • ✓ Indexed across PD modules, AI tools, library, committees, orientation, and quick actions.

Smaller upgrades worth a mention

📚 Library Hub split into two views

A chooser landing page now routes students to research tools and AI Gems, and teachers & staff to collection & compliance resources. The student view is locked from the wider Hub to reduce confusion.

A floating Request a Module button now lives on the PD Modules page, and a Request a Tool or Resource button on Tools & Resources — tell us what to build or add next.

🏷️ Real brand logos on AI tool cards

Magic School AI, NotebookLM, Gemini Storybooks, and Chalkie.ai cards now show their actual product logos instead of generic emojis — easier to scan at a glance.

🦁 Orientation Hub for new staff
