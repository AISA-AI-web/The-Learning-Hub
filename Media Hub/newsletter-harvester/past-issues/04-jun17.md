### Year-end roundup — what's new on the Hub, in Workspace, and from ADEK

The Learning Hub now signs you in and remembers your progress, Gemini will run your calendar for you, and a handful of important ADEK dates land in August and September.

June 17 For All Staff

Big Change · Read This One

#### The Hub now signs you in — and remembers what you've finished

The Learning Hub is now email-authenticated. You'll sign in once with your @aisa.sch.ae account, and from then on every PD module, training, and certificate you complete is saved against your name. Close the tab, come back tomorrow, and your progress is still there.

- ✓ Sign in once. Your @aisa.sch.ae Google account is the key — no separate password to remember.

- ✓ Module progress is saved. Start a module, get pulled into a meeting, finish it later from the same spot.

- 🎓 Certificates are auto-generated. Finish a PD module and the Hub builds your certificate automatically — ready to download from your Dashboard for your personnel file. No more chasing anyone for one.

- ✓ SLT is being added as admins. They can see Hub-wide completion data and post school-wide notifications when something matters — like the Chalkie.ai feedback survey landing later this week.

- ✓ Nothing else changes. Same URLs, same pages, same content. You'll just see a Google sign-in the first time you land.

Mobile · Try Today

#### Google Drive mobile — hover your camera, get a clean PDF

The Drive mobile app's scanner just got a serious upgrade. You no longer have to tap, align, and crop — just hover your phone over the document and it captures, AI-enhances, and uploads to your Drive in one motion. Great for parent letters, student work, ADEK paperwork, anything physical you need digital in five seconds.

💡 How to find it

Open the Drive app on your phone → tap the ＋ button → choose Scan. Hold the camera over the doc — that's the whole thing.

Classroom Update

#### Track student progress on learning outcomes & standards in Classroom

Google Classroom now lets you tag assignments against learning outcomes and standards, and view per-student mastery over time. For students: clearer feedback on which skills they're nailing and which need another pass — they can see the picture of their own learning. For teachers: faster intervention for the kids who actually need it, and the data to plan the next unit around where the class is — not where the pacing guide says it should be.

#### MagicSchool now has a podcast creator

MagicSchool has rolled out a podcast-creator inside its teacher suite — turn a lesson, an article, or your own notes into an audio episode for students to listen to on the way home. Personal opinion: worth a look, but I still find NotebookLM far more engaging and versatile for audio overviews — the hosts feel less scripted, the dialogue actually engages with the source material, and you can shape the output with custom prompts.
