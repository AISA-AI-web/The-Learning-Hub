For: teachers delivering the AI Literacy curriculum

#### How often you'll teach it

Teaching starts the week of September 21 — your first lesson falls somewhere in that week, not necessarily on the Monday. ADEK's entitlement is one period a week for KG through Grade 5, and two periods a week for Grades 6–12.

How AISA is delivering it. In Grades 6–12, one period a week is timetabled, and the rest of the entitlement is completed asynchronously. In elementary, nothing changes: K–5 homeroom teachers deliver one timetabled, standalone AI lesson a week.

##### Planning beyond your first lesson?

The full Scope & Sequence maps every grade from KG to 12 — the progression, what students are expected to reach by the end of each phase, and how each strand builds year on year. It's a reference, not a module: dip in when you need it.

#### AI Growth Test: dates coming shortly

ADEK's AI Growth Test is a baseline measure of where students are starting from in AI literacy. Every student in Grades 4–12 takes it, and ADEK's testing window closes on Sunday, October 4. AISA's dates for each grade will be announced shortly.

##### Who runs the test

In Grades 4 and 5, the class’s own homeroom teacher. Grades 6–12 aren’t settled yet — I’ll confirm that along with the dates. Grades K–3 don’t sit this test at all.

If you’ll be running it, ADEK’s AI Lead & Proctor guide is on the Hub: checking your grade’s roster before the day, generating the access code, briefing students, and what to do if something goes wrong mid-test. It’s a reference for when you need it — nothing to work through now.

For: all teaching staff

#### A reminder: keep student details out of AI tools

As more of us use AI for planning, please don't enter student names or other identifying details into AI tools. Describe the learning need, not the learner — write "a Grade 7 student who finds multi-step word problems hard", not the student's name, grades or support plan.

Only tools on the AISA Approved Tools Register may be used for school work — and being on the register is not the same as being approved for student data. Analyzing assessment data, for instance, needs a tool specifically approved for that. The register, the rules and worked examples are all in the AI Vision & Ethical Framework module.

This follows ADEK's School Digital Policy and UAE data protection law.
