New PD Module

#### Chalkie.ai Training — a practical guide is now live

Now that the AISA-wide Chalkie trial is running, we've shipped a full PD module on how to use it well. The module's frame: Chalkie is a strong drafting partner, not a lesson designer — and there are two specific weak spots teachers should know about before sending Chalkie output to students.

- ⚠️ Watch-out 1 — DOK levels: default Chalkie presentations cluster at DOK 1 (recall). Recommendation: plan a DOK-graduated slide outline in MagicSchool or Gemini first, edit it as a teacher, then hand the finished plan to Chalkie to render.

- ⚠️ Watch-out 2 — differentiated worksheets: tiers can drift in length-only or assess different outcomes. Treat every output as a draft and verify each tier still maps to the same learning outcome before printing.

- ✓ Workflow — plan-then-prompt: a 4-stage routine that keeps cognitive design with the teacher and uses Chalkie for what it does best: layout, phrasing, and visual polish.

- ✓ Accountability: final teaching decisions sit with the teacher. You know your students; the AI does not. Anything that goes to students under your name carries your professional accountability.

📦 What's in the module

~20-minute self-paced read · 7 sections · 2 knowledge checks · the plan-then-prompt workflow · a quick-reference Dos & Don'ts card. Linked from the Chalkie tool card too — one click from tool to training.

New PD Module

#### Google Workspace Studio — build AI Flows for the work you do every week

Google has launched Workspace Studio at studio.workspace.google.com — a no-code builder for Flows: Gemini-powered AI agents that watch your Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Chat for a trigger, then run a chain of actions for you. Our new PD module walks through the building blocks (Starter + Steps + Skills) and five worked Flows you can deploy this week.

- ✓ Daily inbox briefing — every weekday at 7:30 AM, Gemini reads everything that arrived overnight, ranks it by who needs what from you, and emails you a one-page summary.

- ✓ Meeting prep brief — The morning of — Studio finds related emails and Drive files, summarizes them against the meeting topic, and saves a one-page brief to your "Meeting briefs" folder.

- ✓ Permission slip filer — Gemini saves the attachment to Drive, extracts the student's name, logs a row in your tracker Sheet, and replies to the parent. Plus a Friday chase-email Flow for the unmatched parents.

- ✓ Skills — call your Flows from anywhere — publish a Flow as a Skill and invoke it inside Gemini in Gmail / Drive / Chat with @your-skill. Module includes a department playbook of 6–8 shared Skills.

- ✓ What you get: ~25-minute self-paced read · 5 worked Flows with example flow editors and outputs · 2 knowledge checks · a Starters & Steps glossary you can print.
